# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful, technically rigorous, and welcoming. Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, avoid harassment or discrimination, and assume good faith while still being precise about technical risks.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or deliberately disruptive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

For conduct concerns, use a private maintainer contact channel rather than a public issue when possible.
